About Referly

A simple way to collect short, confirmed references for your profile.

What is Referly?

Referly helps you collect references from people you work with. You send them a link; they sign in and confirm. No long essays—just a clear, checked line on your profile.

Why Referly?

  • Quick for them: They confirm with one flow after signing in
  • Real people: Tied to their account and their OK
  • Shareable: One profile link you can send anywhere
  • Two-way if you want: You can both add each other on your profiles

Open Source

Referly is built with open technologies and you can view the source code on GitHub. Contributions and feedback are welcome.
