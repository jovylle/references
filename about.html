About Referly

A simple, factual reference verification system for professionals.

What is Referly?

Referly makes it easy to gather verified references from colleagues and collaborators. Instead of asking people to write lengthy testimonials, you simply share a link. They confirm it takes 30 seconds, and your verified connection appears on your profile.

Why Referly?

  • Frictionless: One-click confirmation for your colleagues
  • Verifiable: References are tied to real accounts and confirmed by the person
  • Public: Share your profile URL with employers and collaborators
  • Mutual: Both parties can confirm each other as connections

Open Source

Referly is built with open technologies and you can view the source code on GitHub. Contributions and feedback are welcome.
