Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Introduction

Referly is a reference verification platform. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your data.

Data We Collect

When you sign in with Google, we collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Profile picture (optional)

When you create reference requests, we store:

  • Recipient name and email
  • Position/context information
  • Request status and confirmation data

How We Use Your Data

We use your data to:

  • Authenticate your account
  • Create and manage reference requests
  • Display your public profile
  • Store confirmed references

Data Storage

Your data is stored in Firebase Firestore, a Google Cloud service. We do not sell or share your data with third parties.

Your Rights

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time. Contact us for account deletion requests.

Contact

For privacy inquiries, contact: privacy@referly.uft1.com
